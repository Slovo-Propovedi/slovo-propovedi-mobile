Slovo Propovedi (Слово.Проповеди) is a free and libre open source application for listening to and reading Christian sermons.

Features:
• Audio playback of sermon playlists with background playback and media controls
• FB2 format book reader for offline reading
• User-configurable server URL — no vendor lock-in, works with any compatible backend
• Local notifications only — no proprietary push notification services
• No tracking, no advertising, no analytics
• Russian-language interface
